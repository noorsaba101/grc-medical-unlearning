# Illustrative GRC policies

`GRC-CXR-001.json` defines the proof-of-concept synthetic-marker independence
rule, its approved demo status, marker geometry, and three verification limits.
The hospital authority is explicitly illustrative, not a real clinical approval.
Forget/retain references are **derived by the resolver from provenance**, not
manually supplied policy fields. Generated requests and evidence stay off-chain
under the ignored `results/` directory; only compact hashes/metadata go on-chain.

The thresholds demonstrate acceptance of the reported experiment; they are not
prospective clinical standards. See `blockchain/README.md` for executable commands,
the trusted-verifier boundary, and the historical-run authorization distinction.
